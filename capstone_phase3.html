Before you begin

  • The exam has three sections in order. Each section has its own timer. When a timer ends, the exam moves on automatically, and you cannot go back to an earlier section.
  • Inside a section you may move freely between passages or tasks.
  • Your answers save automatically. If the page closes or refreshes, reopen this same file in the same browser and choose “Resume”.
  • Closed book: no dictionary, translator, AI tool or phone. You may use blank paper and a pen for notes.
  • Copying and pasting are switched off. Please type your own answers.
  • Listening uses an audio cue. You can preview the placeholder audio file below before starting.
  • At the end you will download your answer sheet and upload it to Google Classroom.
